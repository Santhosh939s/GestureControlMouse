Gesture 1

Move Cursor

Raise only your index finger and move your hand to control the cursor.

Gesture 2

Left Click

Pinch index finger + thumb together briefly to left-click.

Gesture 3

Right Click

Raise index + middle fingers and tap their tips together to right-click.

Gesture 4

Scroll

Index + middle spread apart (not touching). Move hand up/down to scroll.

Gesture 5

Click & Drag

Pinch and hold index + thumb for 12+ frames to start dragging. Release to drop.

Gesture 6

Idle / Fist

Close your hand into a fist to pause cursor movement.
